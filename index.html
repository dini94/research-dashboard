1 Research Background & Introduction

Global energy consumption continues to escalate, driving the urgency for sustainable photovoltaic technologies. Dye-Sensitized Solar Cells (DSSCs) have received widespread attention due to their eco-friendliness, ease of fabrication, and indoor light-harvesting potential.

Designing novel sensitizers through trial-and-error chemical synthesis is time-consuming. Computational quantum chemistry—specifically Density Functional Theory (DFT) and Time-Dependent Density Functional Theory (TD-DFT)—provides an invaluable in-silico platform to predict frontier molecular orbital (FMO) distributions and theoretical photovoltaic metrics.
